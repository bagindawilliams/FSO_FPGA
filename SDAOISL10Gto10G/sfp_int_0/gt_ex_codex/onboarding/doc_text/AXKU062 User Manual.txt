Part 1: FPGA Development Board Introduction
    The entire structure of the development board is designed by inheriting our
usual core board + expansion board model. Use high-speed inter-board
connectors to connect between the core board and the expansion board.
    The     core   board   is    the   smallest   system   mainly   composed   of
XCKU060-2FFVA1156I + 4 DDR4 SDRAM + 2 pics of 128Mb QSPI FLASH.
    The carrier board expands a wealth of peripheral interfaces for the core
board, including 2*10G SFP+ Interfaces, 3 FMC Interfaces (1 HPC, 2 LPC), 1
Gigabit Ethernet, 1 UART serial interface, and 1 SD card Interface, LEDs and
KYEs.

Part 2: ACKU060 Core Board
Part 2.1: ACKU060 Core Board Introduction
    ACKU060 (core board model, the same below) core board, FPGA chip is
based on XCKU060-2FFVA1156I of XILINX company KINTEX Ultrascale +
series.     The   core   board    uses     4    Micron   1GB    DDR4     chips
MT40A512M16LY-062EIT, with a total capacity of 4GB. In addition, two
128MBit QSPI FLASH is also integrated on the core board, which is used to
start storage configuration and system files.
    The 6 board-to-board connectors of this core board extend 359 IOs, 104
IOs of BANK64 and BANK65 are at 3.3V and all other BANKs are at 1.8V. The
core board also has 20 pairs of high-speed transceiver GTH interfaces. For
users who need a lot of IO, this core board will be a good choice. Moreover, in
the IO connection part, the wiring between the FPGA chip and the interface has
been processed with equal length and differential processing, and the size of
the core board is only 80*60 (mm), which is very suitable for secondary
development

powered by the carrier board. The core board power supply schematic is show

    +12V is used to generate +0.95V FPGA core power through the DCDC

through the DCDC chip ETA1471 to generate +1.2V, +1.8V +3.3V and

generated by the DCDC chip ETA8156, and the GTX auxiliary power supply

voltages are generated by the TPS51200.

    The core board expands a total of 6 high-speed expansion ports, using 4
120-Pin inter-board connectors (J1,J3,J4,J5) and 2 80Pin connectors (J2,J6) to
connect to the carrier board. The connector uses using Panasonic
AXK5A2137YG and AXK580137YG, corresponding to the carrier board
connector AXK6A2337YG and AXK680337YG.
    The J1 connector is the signal for BANK66 and BANK68 with a level
standard of 1.8V.

Part 3.4: Gigabit Ethernet Interface
     There are 1 Gigabit Ethernet ports on the AXKU062 FPGA Development
board. The GPHY chip uses Micrel's KSZ9031RNX Ethernet PHY chip to
provide users with network communication services. The KSZ9031RNX chip
supports 10/100/1000 Mbps network transmission rate, and communicates
with the MAC layer of the system through the RGMII interface. KSZ9031RNX
supports MDI/MDX adaptation, various speed adaptation, Master/Slave
adaptation, and supports MDIO bus for PHY register management.
     When the KSZ9031RNX is powered on, it will detect the level status of
some specific IOs to determine its own operating mode. Table 3-5-1 describes
the default settings after the GPHY chip is powered on.

     When the network is connected to 100M Ethernet, the data transmission of

the IO of FPGA BANK65 and BANK66, the user can control the on and off
through the program, when the voltage of the IO connected to the user LED is

Part 3.12: JTAG Interface
    A JTAG interface is reserved JTAG interface one the AXKU062 FPGA
development board for downloading FPGA programs or firmware to FLASH. In
order to prevent damage to the FPGA chip caused by hot plugging, a protection
diode is added to the JTAG signal to ensure that the voltage of the signal is
within the range accepted by the FPGA to avoid damage of the FPGA chip.

Part 3.14: Fan
    Because AXKU062 FPGA development board generates a lot of heat
when it works normally, we add a heat sink and fan to the chip on the board to
prevent the chip from overheating. The control of the fan is controlled by the
FPGA Chip. The control pin is connected to the IO of the BANK48. If the IO
level output is Low, the MOSFET is turned on and the fan is working. The fan
